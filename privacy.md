# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026

This policy covers **SVR Player** (the VR app for Meta Quest, package `cc.mayoia.simplevrplayer`) and **SVR Link** (the companion app for Windows). In this policy, "we" means the developers of SVR Player.

## Summary

We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. The apps have no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything they store stays on your own devices.

## What SVR Player stores on your headset

- Player settings: volume, screen size, distance and height, subtitles on/off, environment, default folder.
- Playback positions and chosen video formats for files you have watched, keyed by file path.
- Video preview images (thumbnails) cached on the headset.
- If you use SVR Link: the ID, name, local network address and PIN of your PC, and an access token issued by SVR Link.

This data never leaves your headset, except as described in "Network use" below. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

- **All files access** (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`): to find and play your video, audio and subtitle files in any folder, and to delete files only when you choose Delete. Files are read on the device and are not uploaded anywhere.
- **Internet / network**: only to connect to SVR Link on your own local network (see below).

## Network use

SVR Player connects only to SVR Link running on your own PC in your local network. It finds the PC with a local UDP broadcast and then requests the folder list, video files and previews over HTTP. This traffic stays inside your local network and is not sent to us or to any third party.

## What SVR Link stores on your PC

- The folders and files you add, the PC name, the PIN, language and autostart settings, and the list of headsets that have entered the PIN (a random device ID and access token). Stored in `%AppData%\SVRLink\config.json`.
- Video preview images cached in your user cache folder.

SVR Link does not connect to the internet and does not send any data to us.

## Purchases and Meta

SVR Player is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. If you buy an in-app item, the payment is processed by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy policy. The app may ask the Meta platform whether you own the app or the item (an "entitlement check"); we receive only the answer, not your payment details. Meta may also collect data about your use of its store and platform under the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

## Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect any data from them.

## Data deletion

Since we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete the data stored on your devices, clear the app data or uninstall SVR Player on the headset, and delete the `%AppData%\SVRLink` folder on your PC. If you have any questions, contact us at the address below.
