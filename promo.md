# Free Premium Promo Codes (Creators & Community)

Want **SVR Player Premium** for free? We are giving away a limited number of **single-use promo codes** to creators and community members!

## How to get a code

1. Post a short video, review, or showcase of **SVR Player** on **YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, or Facebook** (honest feedback is always welcome). *Note: Meta Horizon Store reviews are not eligible — external social media posts only.*
2. Send the link to your post or channel to **elfenliedgames@gmail.com** (subject: `SVR Promo Code`).
3. We will reply with a **single-use Premium promo code** while supplies last.

*YouTube / TikTok VR creators can also email us before making a video to request a review code.*

## How to redeem your code

1. Install the free **SVR Player** from the [Meta Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/1407967885740014/).
2. Open the app and tap to upgrade to **Premium**.
3. In the Meta Quest checkout window, tap **Add promo code**, enter your code, and click **Apply**.
